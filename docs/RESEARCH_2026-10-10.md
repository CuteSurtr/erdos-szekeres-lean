## New geometric constraints and their scope

`Omission.AdditionalResidualRows.all_rows` applies to a `Configuration n` of
real points in general position ordered by strictly increasing x-coordinate,
with no 6-cap, no convex octagon and no incoming-6-cup/outgoing-5-cap junction.
These are exactly the residual hypotheses used by the completed partition.
The existing injective encoding assigns points to 154 possible states.

The new constraints are:

- Twenty explicitly ordered eight-state cycles, each requiring at least one
  of its states to be unoccupied.
- One 28-state block, requiring at least six unoccupied states. The checked
  compression satisfies the hypotheses of the already formalized Baek bound
  of 22 points for the relevant 4-cap/octagon-free subconfiguration.

The literal cycles, block support and compression thresholds live in
[AdditionalResidualRows.lean](../ErdosSzekeres/Omission/AdditionalResidualRows.lean).
`cycles_checked` and `block_checked` use kernel reduction; `cycle_hole_bound`
and `block_hole_bound` connect the finite data to actual points. The search
programs and numerical optimizers are outside the trusted proof.

The separate theorem
`AdditionalResidualStrictness.independent_each_additional_row` supplies, for
**each** added row, an abstract occupied set satisfying all 1,187 original
rows and the other 20 added rows while violating that row. Cycle witnesses
occupy just the eight cycle states. The block witness occupies 23 states and
leaves exactly five of its 28 states missing. This establishes strict
strengthening and irredundancy of the augmented Boolean model. These witnesses
are not asserted to be realizable by points; the geometric theorems rule out
such residual realizations. See the [proof artifact and reproduction commands](../experiments/omission-additional-rows-2026-10-10/README.md).

## Search results and limits

The cycle discovery traversal visited 264,941 sequences and found 2,144
certified eight-state families. Testing whether any single original row
already forbids fully occupying each family leaves exactly the 20 above.
The original discovery program used support containment as a shortcut; a
second pass used the precise implication test `|R \ S| < demand(R)` and
returned the same 20. This is a reported finite search outcome, **not a
Lean proof of exhaustive discovery** or of completeness among all possible
geometric inequalities. The block scan examined 1,000,000 threshold systems,
recovered all 23 original supports and found the additional support.

## Exact barrier in the current asymptotic proof

Write `C* = 8√2/3`. Our already proved limiting normalized entropy slack at
fixed positive scale `c` is

`L(c,C) = 3cC − 16c² − 2`.

[ParameterBarrier.lean](../ErdosSzekeres/HMPT/ParameterBarrier.lean) now proves

`L(c,C) = 3c(C − C*) − (4c − √2)²`.

Consequently `positive_leading_slack_iff` establishes
`(∃ c > 0, L(c,C) > 0) ↔ C* < C`. At `C*` the only scale with nonnegative
slack is `c = √2/4`. For every fixed positive `c` and `C < C*`, the exact
normalized budget is eventually negative
(`budget_eventually_negative_below_published`).

This is an elementary optimization of the **implemented fixed-scale proof
family**, not a new extremal lower bound. A brute-force search over this
parameter cannot improve its coefficient. An improvement needs a stronger
geometric extraction, a better entropy/counting inequality, or a genuinely
different asymptotic argument. The theorem does not exclude those possibilities
or settle the zero-leading-slack endpoint using lower-order terms.

the old 30-hole exclusion certificate and final linking. The geometric transfer
