## Why

The five H3/one roots `0s0_1` … `0s0_5` (the hull and the second layer are both triangles) are the last hard part of the 3-fold class. Their six-bit σ-cubes do not close at 1800 s, and their layer children whose third layer is again a triangle take about 1700 s each (`results/C3COMP1/README.md`, `results/C3H1/X2/`). Split once more on the σ bits of residues 8, 9 and 10, a six-bit cube's 8 nine-bit cubes all closed in 450–662 s each (`results/C3H1/X1/README.md`). So this wave solves the nine-bit cubes of the first 239 of the 319 open six-bit cubes (`results/C3W7/PLAN.md`), once each at cap 900, and splits nothing further. Their σ-split records (six-bit cube → 8 nine-bit cubes on residues 8, 9, 10, the 3 smallest free residues) are in `results/C3W7/presplit.jsonl`, written without a solve; a split claims nothing, and `compose.py` checks its bits (`results/C3U/sigma_split.md`, rule 2). The other 80 six-bit cubes are being done the same way on a local machine (`results/C3H1/LOCAL/`).
