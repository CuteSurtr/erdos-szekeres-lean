## Why

After waves 4 and 5 the open work of the H3/two and one-orbit roots is in six-bit σ-cubes (node names `L__C` with 6 bits in C; `results/C3COMP1/README.md`). At cap 300, 63% of the attempted ones timed out, and the layer split of a timed-out cube costs about 51 children of about 50 s each. Solved alone at cap 1800, all 15 timed-out cubes of those roots that were tried closed in 240–367 s (`results/C3COMP1/README.md`, cap experiment): the cap was just below the typical solve time. So this wave solves each open cube once, at cap 900, and splits nothing further. Roots that had not been σ-split yet were split by `results/C3W6/presplit.jsonl`: σ-split records exactly as `close_tree.py` writes them (bits = the 3 smallest free residues, 8 children), written without a solve. A split claims nothing, and `compose.py` checks its bits (`results/C3U/sigma_split.md`, rule 2). The five H3/one roots (two nested triangles, whose cubes did not close even at 1800 s) are not in this wave; they are in `results/C3H1`.
