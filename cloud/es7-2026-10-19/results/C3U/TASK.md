## Specification

Make `results/C3U/tools/`, a copy of `results/C3T/tools` at commit ff9b09ee (commit the unmodified copy first), with only these changes.

1. **Names and CNF of cube nodes.** A node name is either a layer name as now (`L`, e.g. `0s0-1s1_2s0-9s0`) or `L__C`, where C is a non-empty list of fixed σ bits written `r s v` (v ∈ {0, 1}), joined by `-`, sorted by residue, e.g. `0s0-1s1_2s0-9s0__3s0-4s0-5s1`. The CNF of `L__C` is the CNF of `L` exactly as now (base + the layer units of `layer_clauses` in their current order) followed by one unit per bit of C in the order of C: `E.sig(r)` if v = 1, `-E.sig(r)` if v = 0 (the same units and order as `sigma_cubes.py`). `units` counts all units.
2. **σ-split.** New option `--sigma-split K` of `close_tree.py` (default 0: no σ-split, behaviour identical to C3T) and `--sigma-levels S` (default 2). A node `L` or `L__C` that times out (UNKNOWN within its cap) is σ-split if L has at least 2 layers, |C| < S·K and at least K residues are free, where free means: r ∈ {1, …, m − 1}, r not in any layer of L, r not in C. Its children are the 2^K nodes `L__C'` with C' = C plus the K smallest free residues, over all assignments. Its record: `{"node": name, "split": true, "sigma_bits": [the K residues], "children": 2^K}`. Otherwise the node gets the layer split as now. The depth condition matters for soundness: the H3 mirror reduction (Lemma 5) and `--mirror-h6` reduce the children of depth-1 nodes by mapping a set S to its mirror image M(S), which changes σ values, so M(S) need not lie in the same cube; σ-cubes must therefore never be placed at depth 1, and the mirror reductions are never applied to the children of a cube node (at depth ≥ 2 `tree_children` applies none).
3. **Layer split of a cube node.** The children of `L__C` are `L'__C` for every L' in `tree_children(L)` (with the same mirror options as for L), except the L' whose newest layer gives a residue r of C an explicit σ different from C's value (those cases are empty; a one-orbit layer gives no σ and never conflicts). The split record is as now (`"split": true`, no `sigma_bits`).
4. **Caps.** A node's cap is `caps[min(d, len(caps) − 1)]`, where d is the number of splits (layer or σ, including pre-splits) on the path from the run's root to the node. For layer nodes without cubes this is the current rule.
5. **compose.py.** Accept the new names and records. A node is covered if it has a certified record whose `cnf_sha256` equals the CNF re-derived from its name, or if one of its split records has all its children covered: for a σ-split record, check that `sigma_bits` are exactly the K smallest free residues of that node (K = their number) and require all 2^K cube children; for a layer split, the children of rule 3. Records from `sigma_cubes.py` (format of `results/C3SIG/*/out.jsonl`: `node` is the parent, `cube` maps residues to values) are read as records of the node `node__C` with C from `cube`; a node all of whose 2^K cubes (on its K smallest free residues) are certified in such records counts as σ-split. `--mirror-h6` and all checks of the C3T version stay.
6. **Soundness note** `results/C3U/sigma_split.md`: why the cube children of rule 2 cover the parent (they exhaust the assignments of the K bits), why the children of rule 3 cover a cube node (the layer split covers L, and the dropped L' contradict C), and why adding units never loses a set of the class.
