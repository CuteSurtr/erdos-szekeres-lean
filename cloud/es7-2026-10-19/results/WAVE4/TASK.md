# WAVE4 task (helpers that close the last open cubes of mirror sub-case (ii))

Your prompt gives four values: V (your machine name, for example H07; v is the same in lower case, h07), H (your time budget in hours) and Z (the commit of this file). Below, replace {V}, {v}, {H} and {Z} by those values. Everything else in this file is to be followed exactly.

General rules:
- Start: `git fetch origin es7-cloud && git checkout es7-cloud`. Record the environment (`uname -a; nproc; free -g || true; df -h /opt || true; date -u`) into `cloud/es7-2026-10-19/results/WAVE4/{V}/env.txt`, then commit and push it at once.
- Time budget: {H} hours of wall time. Commit and push progress at least every 30 minutes. Use the last 15 minutes to write `results/WAVE4/{V}/README.md`, push, and report. If all your cubes are closed earlier, write the README then and end.
- Do not end your turn while work is running. ScheduleWakeup does not fire in this environment. Keep the session active: wait with background until-loops or Monitor that return on progress, commit, then wait again.
- Commit only to branch es7-cloud, and only inside `cloud/es7-2026-10-19/results/WAVE4/{V}/`. Use plain commit messages with no AI co-author or attribution trailers. Run `git pull --rebase --autostash` before every push, and retry the push until it succeeds (many machines push to this branch). If a push still fails after 10 tries, push to branch `es7-fallback-w4-{v}` and say so.
- Never commit files over 50 MB, and never commit CNF or LRAT proof files. Use all 4 cores.
- A cube counts as closed only if cake_lpr prints the exact line `s VERIFIED UNSAT` on its LRAT proof. Do not use drat-trim's lrat-check (unpatched it is unsound).

Background (paths under `cloud/es7-2026-10-19/`):
- Mirror sub-case (ii) is the CNF F = `m33k7int.sub.cnf` (sha256 69c1dc8f7818cbef615c1c6f65db66463294ea8054f0cec602846f27ffff932d). It is split into 450 hull cases (`results/Mii-S/cases.jsonl` has case, U, L and cnf_sha256 for each). The CNF of a case is `python3 results/Mii-S/scripts/hullsplit.py case F.cnf U L OUT.cnf`, with U and L written as comma-separated labels (an empty string for none); its sha256 must equal cnf_sha256.
- Waves 1-3 (`results/WAVE1/`, `results/WAVE2/`, `results/WAVE3/`) closed cases by cube-and-conquer: `march_cu CASE.cnf -d 7` gives 128 top cubes; each cube is solved by CaDiCaL with a 300 s cap and proof-checked by cake_lpr; a cube that times out is split with `march_cu` (split depth 6) on the case CNF plus the cube, and so on. 270 of the 278 wave cases are closed. You close a share of the open cubes of one of the remaining cases. The machines still running on that case keep the cubes at the front of their queues; your list holds cubes they will not reach before their time runs out.
- Read `results/Haudit/AUDIT.md`. Its BUG-1, RISK-1 and RISK-3 describe driver mistakes you must not repeat.
- Every leaf you report will later be re-solved from scratch by `verify/v3/v3_check.py`, and each case's cover re-checked, so the aim here is to close cubes fast and to record exactly which cubes were closed.
