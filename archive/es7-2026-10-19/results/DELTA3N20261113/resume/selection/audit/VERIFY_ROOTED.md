## Statement, inherited assumptions and projection

Let S have m x-ordered points/signotope labels, no five-cap and no convex
n-gon. Let r=m-1. A rooted weak Delta contains r, has no four-cap and meets
every convex (n-1)-set. This is stronger than the unrooted existence claim.
The filter supports `5 <= n < m <= 16`, including m=n+1.

The unchanged compact encoder has exact convexity flags and sound one-way
cap/target containment witnesses, with a simultaneous actual-containment
canonical assignment. Its prior independent review is
`../../audit/VERIFY_COMPACT.md`; those geometric clauses are not newly
re-audited as a clause family here.

For an orientation assignment, any model of the filtered CNF witnesses
failure of every retained rooted Delta: a true cap flag entails an actual
four-cap in Delta; a true target flag entails a target in its complement.
Conversely, if every retained rooted Delta fails, assign every auxiliary
flag its actual semantic value. The unchanged non-pool clauses and all
retained failure clauses hold simultaneously. An unrooted working Delta
does not obstruct this assignment because its failure clause was removed.
Thus the orientation projection is exactly failure of all retained rooted
Deltas, not merely a consequence of that property.

Write h=m-n+2. Since n<m, h>=3. If a working rooted Delta has size below
three, extend it to three points while retaining r. It is four-cap-free
by cardinality and cannot lose a target it already hits. If it has size
above h, take an h-subset containing r. It remains cap-free by heredity,
and its complement has only n-2 vertices, hence no target. These operations
give a working rooted Delta in `3 <= |Delta| <= h` from any working rooted
Delta. The converse is immediate. Consequently the reduced range has the
same existence property, including its root condition.

At m=n+1 the retained range is exactly size three. Its failure clauses are
empty: neither a three-set nor its (n-2)-point complement can contain the
respective obstruction. This correctly makes the rooted-counterexample
formula UNSAT. The filter rejects m<=n instead of applying a wrong mask
count to the compact encoder's separate empty-range output.

The equivalence with a safely extendible old Delta also holds: remove r
from a rooted Delta to get a weak Delta on S-r; every old target avoids r
and therefore retains another hit. Conversely, adjoining r to an old weak
Delta hits all new targets automatically, provided the resulting set is
four-cap-free. This argument needs no unproved endpoint-choice assertion.

The filtered formula is a literal subformula of the original one. That
fact alone only transfers UNSAT to the original unrooted formula; the
stronger rooted interpretation relies on the projection and range proof
above. No all-size statement follows from a fixed-size certificate.

## New fixed-coordinate finder layer

Reviewed `../rooted.cpp` SHA-256:
`39c2f8466c04e145012436eedab9018bc94e50bf36672d461b5d36dac9ab15af`.
Inherited instrument SHA-256:
`aa9445486c53db9a9217f35a14678fc3b64c7f7d212496932070c263932f1608`.
The unchanged geometry/DPLL infrastructure is inherited, not newly certified
by this review. The added logic is sound for its intended n7/n8 inputs:

1. Geometry sorts by x, so the root bit selects the actual rightmost point,
   even when input rows are reversed. The positive singleton clause forces
   root membership; all negative four-cap clauses enforce cap-freeness.
2. For a candidate Delta, the finder examines chains in its complement.
   An upper cap and lower cup with common endpoints have disjoint interiors
   and their union is convex. The ambient no-n-gon premise bounds every such
   union by n-1 vertices. Thus a union meeting the `>=n-1` threshold has
   exactly n-1 vertices; the explicit size assertion is justified.
3. Each newly added positive clause is a genuine target disjoint from the
   candidate. Any actual rooted solution must hit it. A previously stored
   hit clause cannot be violated by a model satisfying the stored formula,
   so a remaining violation supplies a genuinely new clause. No symmetry
   or arbitrary blocking clause is added.
4. The no-new-clause exit additionally checks the Delta with the independent
   forward recurrence and requires its root bit. UNSAT of a partial hitting
   formula implies no rooted Delta, but the internal DPLL verdict remains
   COMPUTED until a matching exported coloring CNF is freshly certified.

The exported negative candidate CNF includes the root singleton in the
positive-clause vector. The generic artifact's `mode=weak` does not remove
that unit: its interpretation remains rooted. Future negative artifacts
must have all geometric clauses independently justified and include this
root unit; none is silently a refutation of ordinary weak Delta.

A fresh build passed **14 valid small-real controls** and **four invalid
premise controls**, checked with direct hulls and exhaustive masks. Two
valid controls deliberately reverse input order. Valid sizes are 4,8,10,12;
nontrivial n7 cases take two and three lazy rounds and return Deltas
`{4,11}` and `{8,11}`. Both returned subsets independently contain the
