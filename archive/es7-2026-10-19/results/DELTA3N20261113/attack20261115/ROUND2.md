## Definitions (from delta:attack:2)

- Valid: general position, distinct x, no 5-cap, no convex 7-gon. A gon is a convex hexagon.
- Weak Δ of U: a 4-cap-free subset that meets every gon of U.
- h_U(b,c): the length of the longest cap of U ending with the pair (b,c).
- A is terminal-safe in U: A has no 4-cap, and every 3-cap a < b < c in A has h_U(b,c) ≥ 4.
- CONJ-TS′(7): if S is valid and its rightmost point r is the last point of a 4-cap of S, then U = S − r has a terminal-safe weak Δ.
- By Theorem 3 of delta:attack:2, CONJ-TS′(7) at |S| = 16 together with weak D(5,7) for |S| ≤ 15 (CERTIFIED, DELTA2N) gives weak
  D(5,7) at 16. Both verifiers of Task 1 checked this step. So |S| = 15, 16 are the next useful sizes.
