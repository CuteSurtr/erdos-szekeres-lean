# Proposal: regenerate certificates instead of storing them (19 Nov 2026; for the owner's decision)

**Nothing here has been applied to existing data.** The house rule stays in force until the owner decides: keep
every proof (zstd) until an independent check has re-run it.

## Evidence that the big artifacts are reproducible bit for bit

| artifact | test | result |
|---|---|---|
| CaDiCaL 2.1.3 LRAT proof | Δ weak (5,8) m = 15: the CNF regenerated by the record's own encoder, then the solver re-run on 19 Nov | CNF SHA-256 equal to the record's (`228120ee…`); **LRAT stream SHA-256 equal to the one recorded on 13 Nov** (`05636cc8…`) for the proof that was later deleted; cake_lpr `s VERIFIED UNSAT` |
| CaDiCaL LRAT, second case | Δ weak (5,7) m = 15 | CNF SHA-256 equal (`b53d6fb0…`); proof stream hash compared in VERIFY_DELTA2N_RECERT1119.md |
| balanced budget-65 context tree (graft65.py + fastbb256) | context 2464 generated three times | decompressed JSON byte-identical (`28a39c77…`); only gzip's header time differed (now fixed: mtime 0) |

## Proposed rule (replaces "keep the proof until an independent re-run" for regenerable artifacts)

1. **Record everything needed to regenerate.** For every certified CNF or tree, record:
   - the generator and its SHA-256, with the exact command;
   - the input hashes;
   - the solver binary SHA-256 and its options (single-threaded, no time-based options);
   - the SHA-256 of the proof or tree stream.
2. **Check while generating.** Proofs go through the streaming certifier (`cad_stream_cert.sh`: zstd on the fly, the
   stream is hashed, then cake_lpr reads it), so no raw LRAT is ever written. Trees go to both checkers.
3. **Delete only after an independent regeneration.** Once a second run, ideally on another day, has regenerated the
   same stream hash and the checker has accepted it again, the stored copy may be deleted. What is kept: the hashes,
   the generators, the inputs, and the small results.
4. **Keep some proofs regardless.** Proofs behind a published or claimed bound are kept (zstd) until the owner says
   otherwise. A dated list of deleted items with their hashes goes into the record.

## What it would free (estimates from `data/` on 19 Nov)

| class | size now | after the rule |
|---|---|---|
| LRAT proofs (zstd, 481 files) | about 189 GB | about 0 after regeneration checks; each regeneration costs the original CPU time |
| blrat proofs (zstd) | about 23 GB | about 0 likewise |
| CNFs that encoders regenerate | about 146 GB (N66 cubes, lb-k9oct, ...) | about 0; the encoder plus its inputs are kept |
| budget-65 trees (when the campaign ends) | about 84 GB (gzip) | about 0 after both checkers pass, plus one regeneration test per sample |

**The trade.** Disk space for CPU time on re-verification: about 350 CPU-h to regenerate all budget-65 trees, and
the original solve times for the proofs. Certificates behind ES(8) ≤ 307 and ≤ 306 should probably stay stored until
the results are refereed.
