## 1. Exact labelled chirotope (EXACT; own method, different from the filer's interval arithmetic)

`v3_z7chi.py` computes the sign of det(b−a, c−a) for each point Σ w_j ζ^j, using only integers.
- It works in the group ring Z[C7], which maps onto Z[ζ]. It forms E = (ū v − u v̄)(ζ − ζ⁻¹) = −4 sin(2π/7)·det, which is a real number.
- It rewrites 2·E = P + Q·r1 + R·r1² with integers P, Q, R, where r1 = 2cos(2π/7) is the root in (1,2) of
  x³ + x² − 2x − 1.
- det = 0 iff P = Q = R = 0, because 1, r1 and r1² are linearly independent over Q.
- Otherwise the sign is decided with a rigorous integer bisection enclosure of r1 at 256 bits (2,048 in reserve).
- Self-test: the sign agrees with a floating-point oracle on 2,000 random weight pairs, and an exactly collinear
  triple gives 0.

## 4. Soundness and invariance (PROVED; my reading of §3)

I re-derived each step.
- **R2:** Cramer's rule gives u_{i+1} = λu_i + μu_k with λ, μ > 0. det(u_x, p − a) = ε_x·orient(a,x,p).
- **AC:** the barycentric sign argument; the three literals match the three edges' third vertices.
- **OCT:** the visible edges form a cyclic interval. If only e_i is visible, Q ∪ {p} is a convex octagon. A clause is
  violated exactly in that case, with sees(x,y) = orient(x,y,p) < 0 for a ccw polygon.
- **Invariance:** AC and OCT depend only on signs. For R2, the ε-weighted vectors of any realization keep the three
  determinant signs.
- The 7-fold sets have the same labelled chirotope as the encoded integer realizations (part 1), so every point in
  general position that is added to a Z[ζ7] set gives a model of a CERTIFIED-UNSAT CNF. There is none.
