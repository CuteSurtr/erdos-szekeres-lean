# REPLAY: hash-bound replay of retained certificates

Local runs on an Apple M5 Pro, 1 November 2026 (UTC), 14:10–18:02, against `es7-cloud` at 471d5f63a.

**Status.**
- All 191 manifest entries passed the hash check: archive, proof and CNF.
- lrat-check was replayed on the 18 entries whose proofs are under 2 GB.
- Both checkers were replayed on hash-checked files for c5-L1, c3hex-15, c3hex-16 and c4cut-final.
- No checker was replayed on the other 173 entries. Among them are four critical-path certificates: central34-cover,
  mirror33, mirror34 and c5-P1 (§4).

**Why.** The completeness review of `AUDIT3` (A8) found that no retained certificate on the critical path had been
replayed by an independent party with logs tied to the input hashes. This kit is run by the project and makes no
claim of independence.

  - `repo:<path>@9cf4949a5`: committed (the commit is 471d5f63a since `SCRUB20261109`);
  - `archive-self:<file>`: written into the archive by the job that made it;
  - `live:<file>`: records of that night's local re-solve jobs, not committed here.
- **Entries.** 191 in all:
  - central34-hull 120, central34-resample 34, central34-pilot-orbits 20, central34-pilot-resolve 7;

  - c3hex 2, central34-cover 1, C4CUT 1.
- **Content of an entry.** Archive SHA-256 (and size, where recorded), proof SHA-256 and size, CNF SHA-256, and the

  plus unit clauses exactly as the lane built it, or a rebuild of the hull cover or of the C4 cut CNF.
- **`plan.py`** gives the memory and time plan per entry.

## 2. Method (`replay.py`)

**Steps.** For each entry:
1. Hash the archive.
2. Decompress it and hash the proof.
3. Produce the CNF by the first method, hashing while writing, and require all three recorded SHA-256 values. The
   other CNF methods run as cross-checks and must give the same hash.
4. Run lrat-check, then cake_lpr, on exactly those two files.
5. Re-hash both files afterwards.
6. Write one JSON line with the hashes, command lines, exit codes, verdicts, timings, maximum RSS and the SHA-256 of
   each checker's output.

**Modes.** `hash` runs steps 1–3 only, `lrat` runs everything except cake_lpr, and `full` runs everything.

**Controls and tools.**
- Each run with a checker first runs five controls: a good and a bad tiny proof, a forged empty clause without hints,
  the genuine c3hex-15 proof, and that proof with the hint of its last empty clause removed. Each checker that the
  mode runs must accept exactly the good ones. All five behaved as expected: on lrat-check in the lrat run, and on
  both checkers in the full run.
- In both checker runs, the SHA-256 of cadical, lrat-check and cake_lpr equalled the manifest's. The hash runs ran
  no checker and recorded no tool hashes.

## 4. Not replayed

**The four critical-path entries** were not run because of their memory needs:
- central34-cover has a recorded cake_lpr heap of 20,000 MiB (planned footprint 21.4 GB).
- mirror33, mirror34 and c5-P1 have 30,000 MiB each (planned footprint 31.9 GB).
- Their archives, proofs and CNFs are hash-verified (§3), but their checker runs remain the solve-time runs (mirror34
  also has an untracked lrat-check replay of 29 October).

**The other entries without a full replay.**
- 14 had lrat-check only (§3): c5-L2, two pilot re-solves and 11 resample units. c5-L2 is a second proof of the c5-L1
  CNF, and cake_lpr has never been run on it.
- 169 had the hash check only: mirror34-finalcnf (the mirror34 proof on the CNF of the final `zcnf.py`) and 168
  central-34 units with proofs over 2 GB (hull 120, resample 23, pilot orbits 20, pilot re-solves 5). The checker runs
  of these central units are those of the jobs that produced them, plus, for the 120 hull proofs, the re-check in
  `C2RECHECK`.

**What still depends on them.**
- The mirror theorems M33 and M34 are now proved in Lean, from a formalization of Baek's theorem and with no SAT
  certificate (`SYMLEAN/mirror`).
- The fivefold case has the replayed c5-L1 proof, which also needs the layer lemma.
- So, of the four, only central34-cover is still needed by the main claim (central 34-point path) without a replayed
  or Lean-checked alternative.

## Reproduction

From `cloud/es7-2026-10-19/results`, with the proof archives under `$AR`, the three tools in `$BIN` and scratch space
`$W` outside the repository and the archives:

```sh
python3 -I REPLAY/replay.py --manifest REPLAY/manifest.json --res . --aroot "$AR" --bin "$BIN" \
  --work $W/work --logs $W/logs --out $W/replay.jsonl --mode full --select c5-L1,c4cut-final,c3hex
python3 -I REPLAY/summarize.py $W/replay.jsonl
```

The manifest holds the SHA-256 of the ARM builds used here. Other builds need `--allow-tool-mismatch`.
